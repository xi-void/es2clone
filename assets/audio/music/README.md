Put your remastered soundtrack here (WAV, FLAC, OGG/Vorbis, MP3), or use `tools/import_music.py` to link an existing local album. Track 001 is reserved for level startup; other tracks are discovered recursively. Hidden metadata is skipped. Configure order, transitions, menus, volume and the startup cue in `../audio.ini`. No music is generated or downloaded. Recordings are ignored by Git and excluded from packages.
